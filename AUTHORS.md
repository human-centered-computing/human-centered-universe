## Authorship model

Human-Centered Universe does not use a single-author model for all future content.

A person may be credited as an author when they make a substantial original creative contribution such as:

- writing an original story or chapter,
- creating original narrative structure or worldbuilding,
- writing substantial dialogue or character material,
- creating an original adaptation that qualifies as a new authored work,
- creating substantial original audiovisual or interactive narrative content.

Translation, editing, software development, review, illustration, narration, research, cultural review, and other contributions are important but may be recorded primarily in `CONTRIBUTORS.md` unless the contribution also qualifies as authorship.

## Attribution principles

Authorship and contribution credit should be:

- based on actual contribution,
- visible and traceable,
- preserved through Git history where possible,
- connected to stable story or asset identifiers,
- independent of organizational rank,
- updated when new substantial authors join the shared universe.

No contributor is required to transfer copyright merely to participate.

Creative contributions accepted into the shared universe are distributed under **CC BY-SA 4.0** unless a specific rights notice states otherwise.

Software contributions are distributed under **AGPL-3.0**.

## Story-level authorship

As the universe grows, each story or creative asset should identify its own authors and contributors in its metadata where technically practical.

Recommended fields include:

```json
{
  "authors": [
    {
      "name": "Author Name",
      "github": "github-username",
      "roles": ["story-author"]
    }
  ],
  "contributors": [
    {
      "name": "Contributor Name",
      "github": "github-username",
      "roles": ["translator", "illustrator", "reviewer"]
    }
  ],
  "license": "CC-BY-SA-4.0"
}
```

This allows the universe to grow without losing authorship provenance.
