# Story Update v0.3 — Tapetum Sentinel and Family Continuity

## BRG-0001 — The Stone Network

The Stone Network now begins with a repeatable low-light drone mission and uses the architecture of the open-source **Tapetum Sentinel** project as the field intelligence layer.

Narrative flow:

1. Drone imagery is captured at repeated routes, altitudes, and viewing angles.
2. Raw evidence remains preserved separately from enhanced imagery.
3. Retinex Tapetum enhancement, task analysis, tracking, mission metadata, and evidence indexing feed local question answering.
4. Maran and the team proceed through evidence-backed questions rather than accepting one automated interpretation.
5. Tapetum Sentinel explicitly refuses unsupported claims such as “ancient computer” or “quantum entanglement.”
6. Drone evidence guides geophysical and 3D scanning.
7. The resulting graph reveals seven subnetworks, a moving center, and a mathematically missing node.
