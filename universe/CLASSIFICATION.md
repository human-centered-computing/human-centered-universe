# HCU 30-Criterion Classification

Each story is evaluated across three centers. Every criterion receives an integer score from 0 to 10.

## HUMAN CENTER
1. Dignity and personhood
2. Agency and choice
3. Consent, privacy and boundaries
4. Relationship, love and care
5. Justice, rights and fairness
6. Responsibility and repair
7. Identity, belonging and culture
8. Emotion and lived experience
9. Meaning, memory and mortality
10. Wellbeing and vulnerability

## LIGHT CENTER
1. Order and structure
2. Knowledge, truth and evidence
3. Visibility and explainability
4. Measurement and standards
5. Safety and protection
6. Predictability and planning
7. Rules and institutions
8. Coordination and efficiency
9. Continuity and preservation
10. Verification and auditability

## DARK CENTER
1. Uncertainty and the unknown
2. Freedom and alternatives
3. Creativity and invention
4. Exploration and experiment
5. Dissent and resistance
6. Rupture and disruption
7. Transformation and change
8. Risk and volatility
9. Plurality and forking
10. Emergence and surprise

Each center therefore has a raw score from 0 to 100. The three raw scores are normalized so the final story coordinates satisfy:

`HUMAN + LIGHT + DARK = 100`

The final percentages express relative narrative position, not quality, morality or correctness.
