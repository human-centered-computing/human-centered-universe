# Reality Weaving

Human-Centered Universe does not treat reality and imagination as opposing narrative domains.

Reality gives imagination roots. Imagination opens unrealized possibilities inside reality. When archaeology, memory, belief, philosophy, engineering and science fiction meet in an Observer's experience, they may form a new field of thought without visible borders interrupting the story.

> Reality is the root of imagination. Imagination is the unrealized possibility of reality. Their meeting creates a new universe of thought.

## Narrative experience

Inside a story or interactive scene:

- reality and imagination may pass into one another without category badges,
- a historical place may become a future interface,
- an artifact may carry memory, metaphor and speculative technology at the same time,
- past, present and possible futures may coexist through Quantum Time,
- uncertainty may remain meaningful rather than being immediately resolved,
- no single cultural or belief interpretation is presented as the only possible meaning.

The transition must feel woven, not segmented. The reader should encounter one experience rather than a lesson divided into labelled knowledge types.

## Invisible trace

Narrative unity does not require the universe to forget where its material came from.

When useful, story metadata may preserve a `reality_weaving` record containing:

- `reality_roots` — places, artifacts, experiences, research or cultural memories that ground the work,
- `imaginative_transformation` — how those roots are transformed by fiction, metaphor or possible futures,
- `emergent_question` — the new question produced by that meeting,
- `provenance` — optional source traces kept for authorship, research and later examination.

This record is infrastructure, not an interruption inside the story. The default reader does not render it as a set of truth-category badges.

## Integrity rule

Reality Weaving is an artistic and philosophical method, not permission to fabricate evidence.

- Sources are not invented.
- Cultural memories are not reduced to one authorized interpretation.
- A fictional relation does not become a verified physical or archaeological claim merely because it uses scientific language.
- Outside the fictional experience, real-world research and engineering claims remain open to evidence, review and correction.

## Three-center transformation

The three centers do not separate reality from imagination. They transform their meeting in different ways:

- **HUMAN** approaches the weave through dignity, memory, relationship and responsibility.
- **LIGHT** approaches the weave through knowledge, pattern, structure and verification.
- **DARK** approaches the weave through uncertainty, plurality, freedom and unrealized possibility.

Every woven story still obeys:

`HUMAN + LIGHT + DARK = 100`

None of the centers owns reality. None of the centers owns imagination.
