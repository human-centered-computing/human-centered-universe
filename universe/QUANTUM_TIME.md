# Quantum Time

Human-Centered Universe does not assume one universal narrative clock.

A story can precede, follow, coexist with, remember, anticipate, reinterpret or echo another story. A recommended observation order exists only to provide an entry path.

The reader's actual sequence of observations forms a personal Quantum Path.

> **Nodes create space. Choices create movement. Observation creates time.**

Quantum Time is a narrative model. It must not be presented as evidence for a new physical law, conscious control of quantum systems, or faster-than-light information transfer.

Git timestamps, build timestamps, deployments and releases continue to use normal chronological technical time.
