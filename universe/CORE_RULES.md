# Core Rules

## 1. Three Cores

### LIGHT — Light Center
Produces order, visibility, safety, explanation and protection.
Its shadow is control, forced transparency and a single measure imposed on everyone.

### DARK — Dark Center
Opens possibility, freedom, uncertainty, creation and dissent.
Its shadow is irresponsibility, fragmentation and escape from consequences.

### COMMON — Common Center
Not an arithmetic compromise between Light and Dark.
It is a relational space built through dignity, consent, responsibility, contestability, repair and plurality.

## 2. No core is absolute good or absolute evil.

## 3. Stories may differ radically.
Connection does not require shared religion, mythology or historical origin.

## 4. Quantum language has two layers.
Established physics claims and narrative metaphors must remain distinguishable.
An "echo" is not automatically evidence of physical quantum entanglement.

## 5. Common Center is not a single ruler.
It is a plural governance space where powers limit and answer to one another.

## 6. The universe is unfinished.
New stories, languages, cultures, timelines and alternative forks may be added continuously.
