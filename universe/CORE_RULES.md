# Core Rules

Human-Centered Universe uses exactly three fundamental centers.

## HUMAN — Human Center
Human meaning, dignity, agency, relationship, responsibility, identity and wellbeing.

Primary question: **What does this mean for the human being?**

## LIGHT — Light Center
Order, knowledge, visibility, protection, structure, coordination and verification.

Primary question: **How can this reality be understood, ordered, protected and verified?**

## DARK — Dark Center
Possibility, freedom, uncertainty, creation, disruption, plurality and transformation.

Primary question: **What else could become possible?**

## State-space rule

Every story node has a relationship to all three centers:

`HUMAN + LIGHT + DARK = 100`

The three numbers locate a story in the HCU triangular state space. They are not moral scores and they do not place a story inside only one category.

## Classification rule

Each center has 10 criteria. Each criterion is scored from 0 to 10, producing a 0–100 raw score per center. The three raw center scores are then normalized to a total of 100.

A coverage audit must flag important narrative elements that the current 30 criteria cannot reasonably represent. A gap is reviewed by humans; it does not automatically create a fourth center.

## Origin rule

`BRG-0002 — First Vibration` is the origin observation node. It is the shared entry point, not necessarily the metaphysically earliest event in every possible interpretation.

## Observer rule

Readers are Observers. Their end-of-story choices accumulate HUMAN / LIGHT / DARK tendencies. The dominant tendency recommends the unread story with the highest corresponding center weight.

The recommendation is never a restriction. An observer may open any available story.

## Quantum Time rule

HCU does not require one absolute narrative timeline. The sequence in which an observer experiences nodes is that observer's narrative time.

**Nodes create space. Choices create movement. Observation creates time.**

This is a narrative architecture, not a new claim about quantum physics.

## Open-universe rule

The universe is unbounded by design. Any observed state may contain finitely many stories, while new stories, cultures, languages, relations, forks and observer paths may continue to be created.

## Reality Weaving rule

Reality and imagination are not opposing narrative domains. Archaeology, memory, belief, philosophy, engineering and science fiction may flow into one another inside a story without visible category labels interrupting the experience.

The universe preserves an invisible trace of important sources and imaginative transformations in metadata when useful. This trace supports authorship, research and later examination; it is not rendered as a border inside the default reading experience.

Reality Weaving does not turn fiction into evidence. Sources must not be invented, cultural plurality must be preserved, and real-world research or engineering claims outside the fictional experience remain subject to evidence, review and correction. A Quantum Echo remains a narrative relation unless a physical relation is independently supported.

See `REALITY_WEAVING.md` for the complete model.
