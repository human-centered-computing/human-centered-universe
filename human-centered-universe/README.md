# Human-Centered Universe

**Human-Centered Universe (HCU)** is an open, living story universe built around three interconnected cores:

- **Light Center** — order, visibility, safety, explanation, protection
- **Dark Center** — possibility, freedom, uncertainty, creation, dissent
- **Common Center** — dignity, relationship, consent, responsibility, contestability, repair, plurality

No core is absolute good or absolute evil. Light is not automatically good; Darkness is not automatically evil. The universe emerges from their relationships, tensions, limits and transformations.

## Why this universe exists

Human-Centered Universe is both a book and an **open thought laboratory**.

Its purpose is to give people a shared fictional space in which ideas can be imagined freely before they are turned into systems, institutions, technologies, rules, cultures or realities.

A contributor may imagine radically different futures about:

- artificial intelligence and technology,
- law, justice and institutions,
- education and science,
- belief, mythology and culture,
- cities, environment and sustainability,
- freedom, security and power,
- family, memory, death and identity,
- cooperation, conflict and social order.

The universe does not require every idea to be correct, comfortable, compatible with the current canon, or acceptable to everyone.

**The right to imagine is broader than the canon.**

A story can question the canon. A branch can test another possibility. A fork can become an alternative universe.

But creative freedom does not remove responsibility for consequences.

> **Imagine freely. Examine the consequences. Preserve human dignity. Transform what harms.**

One of the central aims of the project is to look inside ideas, systems and conflicts that appear harmful, dark or even evil and ask:

- What human need, fear, value or possibility is hidden inside?
- Can what is valuable be separated from what causes harm?
- Can that value be transformed into a human-centered form?
- Can we increase the good without reproducing the harm?

The goal is **not to romanticize evil, normalize harm, or erase moral differences**. The goal is to understand difficult ideas deeply enough to transform them.

In this universe, evil is not defeated only by deleting or silencing it. Where possible, the human need or value buried inside a harmful form is identified, separated from the harm, and redesigned so that the good can grow.

This is also why Light and Dark must remain distinct from simple good/evil labels. Order can protect or dominate. Freedom can create or abandon responsibility. The Common Center exists to keep these forces in accountable relationship.

## Relationship to Human-Centered Computing

Human-Centered Universe is the speculative and narrative layer of the wider **[Human-Centered Computing](https://github.com/human-centered-computing)** community.

The organization develops open research, software, artificial intelligence, education models and human-centered transformation projects. HCU creates a shared fictional space where the social, ethical, cultural and philosophical consequences of such transformations can also be explored.

Real projects may enter the universe as narrative artifacts or future technologies. When this happens, the boundary between:

- real research,
- established scientific knowledge,
- interpretation,
- and science-fiction speculation

must remain visible.

A project such as **Tapetum Sentinel** can therefore exist both as real open-source work and as a future narrative technology inside the book, without confusing fiction with current technical capability.

## Language policy

**Canonical source language: English.**

English is the shared source language for canon, story metadata, core rules and cross-universe references.

Other languages are translation layers. A translation never receives a new story ID; it remains the same story in another language.

```text
BRG-0003
├── content/en.md   # canonical
├── content/tr.md   # reviewed translation
└── meta.json
```

Translation statuses:

- `canonical` — English source text
- `reviewed` — human-reviewed translation
- `community` — community translation awaiting final review
- `machine_draft` — machine/AI-generated draft, not authoritative

A cultural adaptation is **not** a translation. It becomes a new story node with its own ID and explicit connections.

## Read · Explore · Create

- **READ** — enter the canon and move through story links.
- **EXPLORE** — navigate the network around Light, Dark, Common and Bridge nodes.
- **CREATE** — fork, branch, translate, propose a connection, or add a new story.

Git is part of the narrative architecture:

- **branch** = a possibility,
- **fork** = an alternative universe,
- **pull request** = a proposed connection or reality,
- **merge** = a temporarily stabilized shared reality,
- **conflict** = incompatible narratives that must be examined,
- **commit history** = the memory of the universe,
- **release** = a temporarily stabilized version.

**A fork is not failure.**

Creative freedom and canon governance are intentionally separate:

- anyone may explore a possibility in a branch or fork,
- canon remains a shared, reviewable layer,
- disagreement does not have to disappear before creation can continue.

## Culture and belief

> See the similarity without erasing the difference.  
> Explore the connection without inventing a single origin.

Cultural and belief traditions must not be flattened into one doctrine. Similarities are treated as echoes, recurring human questions, or narrative relationships—not as proof that distinct religions, mythologies or cultures share one historical or theological origin.

Quantum language also has two layers: established physical claims and narrative metaphor. A **Quantum Echo** does not by itself mean physical quantum entanglement.

## Core principles

> Different stories do not have to become the same story. Their relationships make them part of the same universe.

> Freedom to imagine does not mean freedom from consequences.

> Human beings are not raw material for any system.

The universe should make room for difficult questions without reducing people to tools, categories, data points or ideological material.

**Creation is unfinished. Read it. Explore it. Question it. Fork it. Transform it.**
