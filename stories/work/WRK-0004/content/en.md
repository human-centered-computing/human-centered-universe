# The Form

The form had boxes: name, date, reason, signature. One box was unnecessary. I asked why.

“It has always been there.”

One day everything was ready. The work was complete. A signature was missing.

Nothing could move.

“Why?”

“Because the form requires it.”

The purpose had disappeared. The procedure remained.

Another day the information was correct but written in the wrong box. I corrected it and reality became acceptable.

Soon we filled forms about forms.

Years later I found an old form with one empty box. It asked: “What is this for?”

Nobody had filled it.
