# The Corridor

There was always movement. People carried folders from one room to another. I used to think movement meant progress.

One day I followed a file. Room one sent it to room two. Room two sent it to room three. Room three returned it to room one.

Nothing had changed. Only the file had traveled.

The corridor was always busy. A busy corridor created the feeling that something important was happening.

At the end was a door. People entered carrying problems and came out carrying forms. The problem often remained.

Years later I understood that some systems do not prevent work. They make work circulate.

A person can walk all day and still remain exactly where he began.
