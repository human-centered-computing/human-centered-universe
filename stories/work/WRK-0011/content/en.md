# The Meal

At noon something changed. Chairs moved closer. People spoke differently. For a short time nobody was a title.

Someone complained about a child. Someone laughed. Someone shared bread. Someone asked whether another person was tired.

Nobody asked for a number.

The table had no hierarchy, at least not for a while.

Then the clock moved. People stood. Titles returned. Desks waited. Numbers waited.

We returned to the rooms as if nothing had happened.

But I remembered.

Human beings became visible when the system stopped measuring them.
