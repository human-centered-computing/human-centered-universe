# The Replacement

The new person arrived on Monday. Someone gave him keys, a password and the files. Everyone said he would learn quickly.

Nobody gave him the reasons.

Why a file mattered. Why a person needed a different explanation. Why a small exception prevented a larger problem.

Those things were not in the instructions.

He made a mistake. We corrected it. Then another. I remembered making the same mistakes when the people who knew the reasons were still there.

“Replacement” sounded simple, as if one person could become another without changing anything.

But people carry invisible maps. When one leaves, the map leaves too.

The new person eventually learned. Then someone else arrived.

The system was good at replacing bodies. It was less good at preserving what bodies had learned.
