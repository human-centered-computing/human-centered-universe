# The Leave

## A Permission

Leave was a strange word.

It meant absence, but also permission.

To leave, I needed approval.

The place could continue without me.

I could not always imagine continuing without the place.

## Accumulation

Some people saved their leave.

Months passed.

Days accumulated.

“Why don't you use them?” I asked.

They smiled.

“Where would I go?”

I did not know whether they were joking.

## The Empty Desk

One week I took leave.

My desk was empty.

Nothing collapsed.

Someone sat there temporarily.

The files moved.

The calls were answered.

When I returned, everything was almost the same.

I had expected to be missed.

Instead, I discovered that absence could be ordinary.

## Freedom

That disturbed me.

If the system could survive without me, why had I believed that I could not live without it?

The question stayed with me.

I did not answer it.

I only began to notice the difference between permission to leave and the ability to leave.
