# In Place Of

A job could be transferred from desk to desk. No one said whether the person taking over the desk would also take over the previous employee's questions.

The chair moved back a little when a new person sat at the table.

The new employee was told about the previous owner's affairs. He learned which keys to press, which folder to look in. No one said what the previous employee wanted to change at that desk. I brought this up to him one day. “Am I also taking over the opinion of the person passing by?” he said. "If you want." He read the old note on the edge of the file. Then he added his own question. Two people talking about the same business without ever seeing each other made me think about something other than changing places in the room for the first time.

There was nothing left over from the previous employee on the table.

There were files.

There was a computer.

There was a pen.

The new person turned on the screen.

I told the newcomer to complete the steps in the file. When asked what they were for, I could not answer.

“What is supposed to happen in the end?”

In the past, I would have answered this question immediately.

This is the way.

I didn't say it this time.

I pulled the file towards me.

“Let's see first.”

On the first page:

Necessary action will be taken.

it said.

Newcomer:

“What is the point?”

"I don't know."

“Who knows?”

There was a name under the file.

We called.

“I don't have this file anymore.”

“So who has it?”

"I don't know."

The phone is turned off.

The newcomer looked at me.

“What do we do now?”

"I don't know."

He was silent for a while.

Then he wrote on the edge of the paper:

From whom will it be learned?

The file went to another table.

The person there:

“I've seen this before.”

he said.

“What was done?”

“A transaction has been made.”

“What process?”

He opened the file.

He looked at the pages.

“It's not written here.”

Newcomer:

“What was supposed to happen in the end?”

The man squared his shoulders.

"I don't know."

The new arrival also wrote this on paper.

The file came to our desk again.

The newcomer had a piece of paper in front of him.

He wrote three titles:

work to be done

expected result

Who will

Filled the first line.

He stopped at the second line.

Then:

The result of the application is clearly visible

wrote.

He wrote his own name on the third line.

“Where did you get this from?”

“From the applicant coming again.”

I looked at the file.

"I think so too."

The next day the file went to another desk.

The person there looked at the new paper.

“Who wrote this?”

The newcomer raised his hand.

"I."

“There was no such area.”

“There is now.”

The man looked at the paper.

“Do we have to fill this out?”

"I don't know."

I intervened.

“Let's talk about what the outcome is first.”

The man opened the first page of the file.

“The applicant needs to be informed.”

Newcomer:

“Can we write this as the expected result?”

The man thought.

"In summer."

The file progressed.

One person checked the document.

Someone else filled in the missing information.

The newcomer gave information to the applicant.

Then a small note was made in the file:

Information has been given.

He wrote the date underneath.

He looked for a while.

“Is this enough?”

"Why?"

“For the result.”

I looked at the file.

“Can we confirm this with what he said?”

The new person took the phone.

They called.

The applicant answered the phone.

“Yes, I received information.”

The new person hung up the phone.

He looked at the file.

He took the pen in his hand.

Then he left.

He didn't write anything.

After a while, one of the old files arrived.

The newcomer opened the lid.

The previous employee's initials were still visible.

“This person did this while working here.”

he said.

"Yes."

“Now I continue.”

"Yes."

“Will what I do replace what he does?”

I looked at the file.

"No."

“So will they both stay?”

“It should stay.”

The newcomer wrote his name next to the old initials.

Then he stopped.

“Let's write down what I did here.”

he said.

He handed me the paper.

I looked for a while.

Then:

“Write.”

I said.

He took the pen.

The old name remained on the page.

The new name was added next to it.

The new person pulled the file in front of him.

“Shall we continue?”

“Let's do it.”
