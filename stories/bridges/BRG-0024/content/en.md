# The Meeting

Meetings were where time went to disappear.

Everyone entered carrying work. Everyone left carrying more work.

The first meetings were long because people had things to say. Later they became long because people had learned how to speak without saying anything.

I once prepared a solution before a meeting. It was accepted in three minutes.

Then someone asked for another meeting to discuss how the accepted solution should be discussed.

By the third meeting, nobody remembered the original problem.

Still, the minutes were perfect.

They recorded who attended, who spoke, what was decided, and what would be discussed later.

I began to suspect that the meeting was not a place where decisions happened.

It was a place where the institution proved that decisions were happening.
