# The Form

The form was only one page.

It asked for my name, date, reason, approval, and signature.

I asked why the same information already stored elsewhere had to be written again.

“Because this is the form,” they said.

The strange thing was that nobody believed the form was useful. They completed it because everyone else completed it.

One day a missing signature stopped an entire process. The work was finished. The result was ready. Only the signature was absent.

People waited.

I watched a finished thing become unfinished because a line on a page was empty.

That evening I asked an older worker whether he thought the form mattered.

He said, “It matters because it matters.”

I wrote that sentence down.

Years later, I realized it was the shortest definition I had ever heard of institutional circularity.
