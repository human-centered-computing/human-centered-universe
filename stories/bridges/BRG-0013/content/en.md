## EXPERIENCE THRESHOLD ◇ — THE REALITY BRIDGE

Choose an idea you want to apply in the real world:

an education model,

an AI system,

a public service,

a healthcare application,

a legal model,

a work method,

an urban solution,

a technology.

First answer:

**What real problem am I trying to solve?**

Then pass the idea through eight gates:

**1. What is different here?**

Why might a solution that worked elsewhere produce different results in this context?

**2. What do we not know?**

What data or perspective is missing?

**3. Who decides?**

Does AI recommend, or replace the decision?

**4. Whose success is success?**

Does improving one metric create loss for someone else?

**5. How will local knowledge meet model knowledge?**

Does lived experience remain invisible?

**6. Who is accountable when error occurs?**

Can responsibility disappear behind the machine?

**7. Who repairs harm?**

Is only the system corrected, or is the human consequence addressed too?

**8. Where is the affected human in the decision?**

Only a source of data?

Or a subject with agency?

After these questions, do not immediately scale.

Where possible:

- build a limited pilot,
- define a clear duration,
- preserve a way back,
- provide independent review,
- measure human impact,
- create a contestation channel,
- record failure,
- repair harm,
- publish the outcome.

Then ask:

**“Is this system growing because it looks successful, or because evidence shows that it is genuinely better for people?”**

Human-Centered Universe does not give us ready-made systems.

**It gives us questions with which we can test systems before they become larger than the people they are meant to serve.**
