# The Number

The number was simple.

Everyone understood it.

More was better. Less was worse.

The number did not know whether a person had been helped, whether a problem had truly ended, or whether the same problem would return tomorrow.

It only knew that something had been counted.

A young man became proud of his number. He checked it every morning before looking at anything else.

Soon he stopped asking whether his work mattered.

He asked whether it would increase the number.

I watched a question disappear and another take its place.

The first was human.

The second was measurable.

The house preferred the second.

So did we, eventually.
