# Governance

Human-Centered Universe prioritizes visible contribution over centralized authorship.

Roles may include: Core Architect, Story Architect, Language Translator, Language Reviewer, Cultural Reviewer, Continuity Reviewer, Classification Reviewer, and Technical Maintainer.

Roles indicate responsibility, not hierarchy.

## Shared-universe review

A proposed story or structural change should be reviewed for:

- human dignity and agency,
- HUMAN / LIGHT / DARK classification consistency,
- cultural and belief-context integrity,
- science / interpretation / speculation boundaries,
- translation integrity,
- contribution visibility,
- technical validity,
- effects on existing story interpretation.

A fork is not failure. An alternative universe may maintain its own internal rules while preserving provenance and contribution history.

Git commits, pull requests, reviews, and merges are the primary contribution record.
